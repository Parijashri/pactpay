How it actually works

Trust & safety, by design.

The product decisions behind the dashboard — not just the metrics.

Ranking logic

Listings aren't sorted by recency alone — proximity and seller trust weigh in too.

score = 0.40 × recencyScore
      + 0.35 × proximityScore
      + 0.25 × sellerTrustScore

proximityScore = 1 − (distanceKm / radiusKm)
sellerTrustScore = (responseRate × 0.5)
                  + (completedExchanges_norm × 0.5)

Spam prevention

  • Phone verification required before a first listing goes live
  • Image-hash de-duplication flags repeated stock photos
  • Rate limits on new listings per account per day
  • Community "report" button feeds a review queue, not auto-removal

Dispute resolution

  • Pickup requires a two-sided confirmation (buyer + seller tap "received")
  • Unconfirmed exchanges auto-escalate to support after 48 hours
  • Both sides rate the exchange — one-sided ratings don't affect trust score
  • Repeated unresolved disputes reduce listing visibility, not an instant ban
